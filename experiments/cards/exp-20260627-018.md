## Findings

Offline materialization (no network) from `data/cache/sec/companyfacts/CIK*.json ::
facts.dei.EntityPublicFloat`, classified by Exchange Act Rule 12b-2 public-float
thresholds ($700M large-accelerated, $75M accelerated):

- 297 canonical-window 10-K/10-Q events; 48 distinct tickers; 46/48 CIKs carry
  EntityPublicFloat. **38 tickers are in the tradeable OHLCV warehouse, 10 are not.**
- **27 filer-status transitions** in full history; **4 inside the canonical windows**,
  all UP-crossings to large-accelerated: CIFR (2025-02-25), CORZ (2025-02-27),
  WULF (2025-03-03), APLD (2025-07-30).
- **0 of those in-window transitions touch a warehouse name.** All 38 warehouse
  periodic filers are permanently large-accelerated (zero transition potential).

Verdict: the cover-page filer-status surface that exp-20260626-008 -> exp-20260627-015
chased via failing network fetches is **materializable offline as a PIT float-threshold
proxy**, but the filer-status-upgrade alpha line is **structurally untradeable in the
core universe** — the signal (float-explosion small/mid names) and the curated tradeable
warehouse (permanent megacap large-accelerated filers) are disjoint.

Caveat: the float-threshold derivation is a PROXY for the cover-page checkbox — it
ignores SEC hysteresis (lower exit thresholds) and the SRC revenue test, and
EntityPublicFloat is Q2-stale relative to the filing date. It is sufficient for the
structural-tradeability verdict, not for an authoritative per-filing status label.

## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? SEC filer category is a deterministic function of public
  float; in the curated megacap warehouse float is permanently >> $700M, so status
  never transitions. The only in-window transitions are crypto/AI-datacenter float
  explosions (CIFR/CORZ/WULF/APLD) outside the warehouse.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Continuing to materialize 10-K/10-Q
  cover-page TEXT for filer-status alpha (exp-010..015 line); float-threshold / status /
  hold / cooldown / notional sweeps; and re-opening via broad-universe expansion (already
  rejected) without first solving the momentum-confound and stale-dating.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? An expansion-approved universe containing
  pre-graduation float-explosion names, plus a PIT field isolating the eligibility
  transition from the coincident price run-up, plus closed forward replacement-value rows.
