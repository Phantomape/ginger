## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? Append-only JSONL masks pointerization: writers
  append after the pointer header and line-tolerant readers skip it, so the
  file looked alive while 1,526 of 1,725 records were invisible for three
  weeks. The exp-20260611-027 guard missed it because of a pointer-size
  short-circuit, now removed.
- Forbidden near-neighbor retry: de-LFS-ing files above GitHub's 100MB hard
  limit; size-based short-circuits in the pointer guard.
- New evidence for retry: log growth requiring an archived-segment scheme with
  reader support (never truncation).
