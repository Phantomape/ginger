## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The interaction allocator diversified away from an RSI control concentrated in NFLX and MCD. Its selections combined stronger Moomoo-flow absorption with higher captured-chain near-put concentration, producing positive mean deltas at H1/H3/H5/H10 and in both H10 halves. The evidence remains uncertain because Moomoo history is retrospective, the options chain is truncated, daily event windows overlap, and the cooldown diagnostic's first half was slightly negative.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not sweep RSI, drawdown, flow, moneyness, top-N, cost, cooldown, or horizon on these same proxy rows, and do not claim the Moomoo-options join as a new source.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? At least 10 additional genuine forward PIT collection dates and at least 20 settled paired disagreement decisions under the unchanged formula, or canonical decision-time flow plus untruncated three-expiry historical PIT chains.

## Results

The fixed complete-case cohort contained 184 ticker-date rows across 24 tickers and 41 dates. The common H10-settled panel contained 34 paired dates; the two allocators selected different tickers on 24 of them.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| H1 | -0.569% | -0.091% | +0.478% | 62.50% |
| H3 | -0.695% | +0.300% | +0.995% | 50.00% |
| H5 | -0.048% | +2.052% | +2.101% | 70.83% |
| H10 | +0.402% | +2.545% | +2.143% | 79.17% |

H10 chronological-half deltas were `+2.259%` and `+2.026%`. Treatment max ticker share was `14.71%`; every leave-one-ticker-out H10 mean remained positive. The descriptive circular moving-block H10 interval was `[-0.481%, +4.616%]`, so it still crossed zero. With a 10-session same-ticker cooldown, H10 delta remained `+2.055%`, but the first half was `-0.059%`.

This is an overlapping event study, not executable portfolio PnL or max drawdown. The H1/H3/H5 rows are diagnostics; H10 was the only predeclared lead criterion.

## Evidence Boundary

- Moomoo rows dated before 2026-07-02 were fetched later and were not historical decision inputs; only four batches were genuinely forward at preflight.
- OnclickMedia snapshots were forward-collected, but retained two expiries and capped strikes. The put feature is a captured-chain OI share, not the document's exact three-expiry full-chain measure.
- No price-stabilization feature was included. This tests the deep-drawdown cohort's flow×put ranking against RSI, not the original full four-factor hypothesis.
