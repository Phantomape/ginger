# exp-20260508-029: RS Acceleration + No-Gap-Chase Shadow Tag

**Status**: observed_only  
**Lane**: alpha_discovery  
**Completed**: 2026-06-06  
**Owner**: claude-rs-accel-analysis

## Hypothesis

Existing trend_long and breakout_long core candidate rows with BOTH:
- (a) improving 20-day SPY-relative strength (rs_accel: rs_current > rs_prior, where both are computed over 20 trading days), AND
- (b) no signal-day gap chase (|open/prev_close − 1| ≤ 3%)

may identify cleaner continuation candidates with better forward replacement value.

## Single Causal Variable

`rs_accel_no_chase` shadow tag (rs_accel=True AND no_chase=True).

## Method

Read-only attribution on 61 closed core trades across 3 canonical windows (late_strong, mid_weak, old_thin), using:
- OHLCV snapshot JSON files (production-available)
- Backtest trade records from canonical EV-matching single-window files (late_strong: 5.1628, mid_weak: 2.1402, old_thin: 0.5911)

For each trade:
- signal_date = entry_date (signal day; execution is next-open)
- rs_current = ticker_ret20 − spy_ret20 at signal_date
- rs_prior = same 20d return shifted 20 trading days earlier
- rs_accel = rs_current > rs_prior
- gap_pct = signal_day_open / prev_close − 1
- no_chase = |gap_pct| ≤ 3%
- tagged = rs_accel AND no_chase

## Results

| Window | Tagged | Untagged | Tagged Mean PnL | Untagged Mean PnL | PnL Lift | Tagged Mean Ret% | Untagged Mean Ret% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| late_strong | 17 | 1 | $6,497 | $6,609 | −$112 | 8.39% | 11.61% |
| mid_weak | 17 | 4 | $4,392 | $862 | +$3,530 | 4.25% | 10.15% |
| old_thin | 22 | 0 | $1,803 | — | — | 2.16% | — |

**Pooled:**
- Tagged: 56/61 trades (91.8%)
- Untagged: 5/61 trades (8.2%)
- Pooled PnL lift: +$1,709 (dominated by 4 low-basis untagged trades in mid_weak)
- Pooled return lift: −4.6pp (tagged underperformed on % return)
- Materiality: **NOT passed** (return lift is negative)

**Untagged trades**: LLY (late_strong), AMD, TSM, DIS, APP (mid_weak).
All 5 failed the `no_chase` criterion (signal-day gap >3%), while all had rs_accel=True.

## Key Findings

1. **Tag is too broad to discriminate**: 91.8% of core trades already qualify. The current strategy signal engine already selects for RS momentum, so nearly all candidates have rs_accel=True.

2. **Gap-chase entries are NOT detrimental**: The 5 gap-chase trades that failed the no_chase criterion had HIGHER percentage returns (10−12%) than the no-chase tagged trades (4−8%). The hypothesis that gap-chase entries are weaker is REJECTED by this data.

3. **Inverse finding**: In mid_weak, gap-chase entries (AMD, TSM, DIS, APP) had lower absolute PnL ($862/trade) vs. tagged ($4,392/trade), but higher percentage returns (10.15% vs. 4.25%). The absolute PnL gap reflects position sizing differences, not signal quality.

4. **Old_thin fully tagged**: All 22 old_thin trades had rs_accel=True AND no_chase=True — the combination was 100% prevalent in that window.

## Decision: observed_only

The shadow tag experiment is closed as observed_only. Key conclusions:
- Do NOT add a gap-chase filter to core entries — data suggests it would REMOVE profitable momentum captures.
- Do NOT add rs_accel as a standalone filter — it is nearly universal among selected candidates.
- The signal engine already implicitly selects for RS momentum by design (breakout_long / trend_long).

## Next Valid Work

If testing gap-chase effect further:
- Need forward replacement value evidence (not just historical PnL comparison)
- Need a materially different mechanism beyond simple open-vs-prev-close gap
- Could test: sector-adjusted gap (stock gap vs. sector ETF gap on same day)
