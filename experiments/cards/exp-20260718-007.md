## Summary

## Frozen Pre-Run Policy Clarifications

- Pair whitelist: `GOOG/GOOGL` (CIK 1652044), `FOX/FOXA` (1754301),
  `NWS/NWSA` (1564708), `HEI/HEI-A` (46619), `Z/ZG` (1617640), and
  `UA/UAA` (1336917). The helper parses the raw official SEC mapping as
  one-CIK-to-many and asserts these identities; it never auto-admits another
  same-CIK security.
- Outcome-blind price-provenance admission excludes `GOOG/GOOGL` from both
  economic replay and the daily eligible-pair policy: its two historical legs
  are not one provider/adjustment vintage, and later hot data revised the GOOG
  leg by about `6bp`. The fixed traded set is therefore the other five pairs.
  GOOG/GOOGL remains identity-audited and can reopen only after both legs are
  reacquired in one batch/vintage and that exp-local panel is hash-bound.
- Signal anchor: freeze the strictly-prior 120-session log-ratio median and
  scaled MAD at the signal close. Entry requires `|robust_z| >= 2.5` and at
  least `1.0%` absolute premium distance from that frozen anchor.
- Maintain at most one open or pending pair. When multiple eligible pairs
  trigger on one close, rank by descending `|robust_z|` and then ascending
  `pair_id`, scheduling only the first for next-session open. Signals seen
  while a pair is open or pending are discarded, not queued.
- After exit, that pair is ineligible for the next ten complete trading
  sessions and may signal again on the following session.
- Funded-close concentration must keep the largest pair-identity share at or
  below `50%` and pair-identity HHI at or below `0.35`. Entry marked gross may
  not exceed the `$10,000` candidate NAV, cash may never be negative, and open
  plus pending concurrency may never exceed one.
- `$5,000` per leg is a cap. Whole-share quantities are downscaled until long
  basis + equal short collateral + entry costs fit the `$10,000` candidate
  account. Short proceeds are not reusable.
- Entry-dollar imbalance must be at most `5%`; feasible whole-share sizing
  maximizes the smaller leg, then total gross, then prefers lower imbalance
  with deterministic share-count tie breaks.
- Exit against the frozen entry anchor: premium distance `<=0.25%`, an
  additional adverse log-spread move of `3%` from entry, or the close of the
  tenth held session (entry session + 9).
- At a historical fixed-window boundary, cancel any unfilled pending entry and
  force-close an open pair at that final session's close with normal exit cost
  and accrued carry, labeled `window_end_force_close`. This is measurement-only;
  the daily paper policy has no artificial calendar-window exit.
- Costs: `45bp` all-in round trip per leg plus `5%` annualized short carry on
  initial short notional for inclusive calendar days / 365.
- PIT/live caveat: the SEC ticker file is a current snapshot, not an
  effective-dated history. Historical evidence is conditional on this frozen
  whitelist and exact contemporaneous bars. No live-ready claim is permitted
  without effective-dated identity plus broker locate/size evidence.

## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The issuer link was genuine, but the premium did
  not converge on the frozen horizon. Of 23 funded pairs, 20 timed out, two
  hit the adverse-spread stop, one was settled at the window boundary, and
  zero reached the convergence band. Gross PnL was already `-$248.04` before
  `$1,001.02` of two-leg costs and `$205.42` of carry, producing `-$1,454.49`
  net and a `-0.4463` standalone aggregate EV.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not sweep the lookback, z
  threshold, 1% admission distance, convergence band, stop, holding period,
  cooldown, pair subset, direction, costs, or 10% weight on this same cold
  panel. Do not consume the five identities one experiment at a time.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different economic
  anchor/gate shape, or at least 35 new pre-frozen prospective settled pairs
  across at least five identities with effective-dated identity and broker
  locate/size. A same-batch GOOG/GOOGL refreeze repairs provenance, but alone
  does not reopen this rejected policy.
