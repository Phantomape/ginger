## Findings (read-only, full fidelity, 61 core trades, all resolved)

Core stack does **NOT** share the FGRS sleeve's chop-loss; sign reversed and
consistent across all three windows.

| Regime | n | mean PnL | win rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| risk_on_trend | 51 | +$3,813 | 56.9% |
| choppy_range | 6 | +$2,991 | 50.0% |
| risk_off_stress | 4 | +$5,615 | 75.0% |

- `Spearman(p_choppy, pnl)` = **+0.116** pooled (per-window +0.119 / +0.082 /
  +0.118, all positive) vs the FGRS sleeve's **−0.324**.
- In `old_thin` (the chop window) 4 chop-entered core trades averaged +$2,908
  (50% win) vs +$1,714 (41% win) for risk-on entries.
- Core enters in chop only 6/61 = 10% of the time (entry filters already
  concentrate in trend regimes).
- Counterfactual: applying the chop soft down-tilt to the whole core book
  **reduces** total PnL by **−$29,048** ($234,851 → $205,803, ≈ −12%).

## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? Core entry gates (breakout / RS / post-earnings
  continuation + price confirmation) already avoid directionless chop, so the
  few core entries that occur in chop are higher-conviction and positive. The
  FGRS sleeve's chop-loss is a property of its broad quality-gated candidate
  pool, not of the book as a whole.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do NOT apply a portfolio-wide /
  core-stack chop down-tilt (it strips +$29k of core PnL). Do not re-slice these
  frozen windows to "confirm" a chop tilt. Do not retune `regime_chop_state`
  constants on these windows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Forward closed rows tagged with
  entry-time regime for the specific default-off sleeves with negative
  `Spearman(p_choppy, pnl)`; a sleeve-scoped (not core) soft tilt validated out
  of sample.
