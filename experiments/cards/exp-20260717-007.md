## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? NVD publication density was broad, but the fixed exclusion removed profitable entries: aggregate EV fell from 6.2057 to 5.9128 and PnL fell by $7,709.43. Mid and old were clean entry-only regressions; late also changed one AMZN add-on checkpoint.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retune the same Initial Analysis cluster threshold, five-session horizon, issuer subset, weighting, or admission cutoff. Repairing the date-only daily parity gap alone is not new alpha evidence.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different exploitability or issuer-financial-materiality source/signal, or at least 30 PIT-correct settled forward issuer events across at least 10 tickers with replacement values.

## Post-Run Source and Density Audit

- The immutable source archive contains 63,557 official Change History rows over 14 pages. Exact `Initial Analysis` + `Added` + `CPE Configuration` normalization produced 651 cluster>=3 issuer-weeks.
- Broad static vendor-map old/mid/late density was 207/221/212 issuer-weeks across 24/28/29 tickers, with top-1 shares 13.53%/10.86%/12.26%.
- The decision-relevant intersection with the frozen 47-name Gate-4 base was only 82/86/85 issuer-weeks across 5/7/7 tickers, with top-1 shares 34.15%/27.91%/30.59%. Thus every window failed the >=10-ticker bar; old and late also failed top-1 <=30%.
- The vendor map is static, not effective-dated. In particular, it emitted three JNPR clusters after HPE's 2025-07-02 acquisition close; these rows were outside the frozen 47-name evaluation base and did not affect the measured Gate-4 deltas, but they prohibit broader reuse without an effective-dated ownership map. HPE's acquisition close is documented in its [official announcement](https://www.hpe.com/us/en/newsroom/press-release/2025/07/hewlett-packard-enterprise-closes-acquisition-of-juniper-networks-to-offer-industry-leading-comprehensive-cloud-native-ai-driven-portfolio.html). The same map also needs an effective boundary for PANW's 2026-02-11 CyberArk close, although no post-close CYBR cluster entered this archive; see the [official announcement](https://investors.paloaltonetworks.com/news-releases/news-release-details/palo-alto-networks-completes-acquisition-cyberark-secure--ai-era).
- Date-only daily parity missed 37 pre-open same-session activations. Rejected daily wiring was removed; the retained helper and runner are replay evidence only.
