## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? `distance()` awards 0.45 for source identity and
  0.15 for gate-shape identity, so any two candidates sharing one source bucket
  start at a 0.60 structural floor above WARN (0.55), and the engine
  hard-rejected on ANY hit >= WARN. Three false-positive classes followed:
  cross-mechanism same-source discovery pairs (0.7576 witness), schema-mandated
  "replacement value" vocabulary text-routing every candidate into the
  forward_replacement_value/forward_attribution buckets (five 0.62-0.63 hits vs
  unrelated attribution families; the PEAD families were themselves misrouted
  there), and stale open tickets / measurement-lane tickets / alias-expanded
  ohlcv projections (five ~0.61 hits). Repair: mechanism preserved in discovery
  priors with cross-mechanism sub-structural waiver; keywords narrowed to
  compound spellings + frozen_families rebuilt and verified; open-ticket priors
  honor the published 7-day in-flight window and exclude measurement_repair
  reservations; inferred/alias-only projection hits require the 0.85
  structural bar while declared-face hits keep reserve-time WARN calibration.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not re-tune WARN_THRESHOLD,
  the 0.85 structural bar, or the 0.2 mechanism jaccard to admit a specific
  candidate; do not reopen the morning continuation candidate (its closeout
  forbids it regardless of this calibration); do not remove more keyword
  spellings without a concrete misroute witness pair.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A demonstrated true-duplicate pair
  that the new rules admit (over-waiver), a mechanism-rename dodge that clears
  both the token-jaccard and structural checks, or the known residual: closed
  frozen-family records carry no economic_mechanism, so once a massive-source
  family closes, cross-mechanism candidates on that source depend on the
  declared-face/structural rules alone — preserve discovery mechanisms in
  build_frozen_families.py if that starts firing.
