## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The exp-20260702-021 inverted-polarity lead replicated
  strictly out of replay on rows the wired daily observer accumulated after the replay
  window closed; the 2026-07-19 park-note flag that the early forward direction looked
  opposite was a 56-row noise read. Intra-theme rotation after adverse first-order news
  remains the surviving story, and the reversal confound was already excluded by
  exp-20260703-005.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Re-running this identical read on a
  same-week ledger refresh; re-slicing the same out-of-replay cohort by relation_type,
  theme, first-order ticker, horizon, matching, or response shape (per-relation-type
  numbers are descriptive only); treating the relative separation as a standalone long
  entry signal.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? The primary next step is the
  shared-paper-first default-off polarity-tilt helper (historical replay + daily
  default-off snapshot + execution envelope) under Gate 1-4. A further attribution
  re-read of this face needs >= 650 closed negative-side out-of-replay rows (+50% and
  >= +10 from 433); `scripts/build_reopen_readiness.py` was synced to 650 in this ticket.
