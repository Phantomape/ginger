## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? Three independent silent stalls (FTD sleeve dead 6
  days, six accepted helpers never persisting, three legacy dirs empty) shared
  one pattern: early-return without persistence. A central read-side report
  over the payloads the daily run already assembles, plus on-disk
  snapshots.jsonl freshness in US equity sessions, covers current and future
  sleeves uniformly without touching any builder.
- Forbidden near-neighbor retry: silencing health warnings instead of fixing
  the sleeve; moving health reporting into individual builders where new
  sleeves can forget it.
- New evidence for retry: per-sleeve cadence overrides if a sleeve legitimately
  emits less than weekly.
