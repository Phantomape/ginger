## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The aggregate sleeve PnL was positive, but the edge was not robust: `old_thin` lost money and added 5.15 percentage points of drawdown, `mid_weak` missed QQQ replacement, two windows lost EV, top-five positive-PnL concentration reached 65.73%, and two selected peers used a future-informed multi-share-class mapping.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retune filing scope, partner definition, fiscal gap, share mapping, industry taxonomy, peer pool, liquidity rank, top1, entry, hold, notional, costs, or issuer subsets on these frozen rows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely new source or gate shape, or at least 30 prospectively settled unchanged-policy decisions with positive replacement value and PIT industry/share-class mapping.
