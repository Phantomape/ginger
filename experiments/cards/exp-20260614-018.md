## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? On the broad insider sample the clustered Form 4
  open-market-buy sleeve was no longer positive: aggregate EV +0.31 (+3.9%, below
  the 10% materiality bar) but PnL -$930 (-0.40%) with 1/3 EV-window regression.
  Sample (33 selected trades) and concentration (single-ticker positive share
  0.165) guards now PASS, so the rejection reflects the true edge, not power. The
  narrow exp-20260508-028 positivity (49 tickers / 234 buys) was a small-sample
  artifact, not a generalizable insider-buy alpha.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retune cluster window,
  owner-count, notional, hold-days, or cost thresholds on this broad sample, and
  do not re-slice to a sub-universe to recover positivity (cherry-picking).
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different insider-quality
  discriminator -- role-weighted CEO/CFO conviction, buy size relative to owner
  holdings, buy-after-decline timing, or pairing insider buys with a confirming
  fundamental/price-state gate -- not a threshold sweep on the same event rule.
