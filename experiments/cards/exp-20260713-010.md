## Gate Decision

Gate 1, source/sentinel Gate 2, and Gate 3 (`22 / 86 = 25.58%`) passed. Gate 4 failed window stability, concentration, and both accepted-distribution comparators. The daily production wiring and parity row were removed; no live/default order, ranking, sizing, or exit behavior changed.

The complete two-configuration, 385-date-aligned DSR panel is retained, but DSR is honestly `not_computable` because the expected-maximum approximation was negative. Gate 5 therefore failed closed without changing Gate 4.

## Post-Run Reflection

- Why: the source was slightly positive in aggregate, but added only `$21.67` per target trade, lost money in `old_thin`, and depended on a narrow positive tail.
- Forbidden retry: do not retune firm aliases/maps, Class I product slices, green/SPY-relative thresholds, rank, cooldown, hold, notional, costs, or window slices on this frozen response.
- New evidence required: a materially new report-time severity or issuer-financial-materiality field with audited PIT availability, or at least `30` closed forward replacement-value rows from a complete daily candidate lifecycle.
