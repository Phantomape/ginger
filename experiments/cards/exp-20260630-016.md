## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The exp-20260627-026 "stretched entries do worse" read rested on only 6 stretched rows out of 41 forward rows. At in-sample scale (2594 breakout entries, 132 stretched) the effect does not just vanish, it reverses: stretched fwd-10d +1.72%/+1.79% raw and +1.38%/+1.19% vs SPY, BETTER than normal; and extension->return is non-monotonic by quintile (Q4 worst, most-extended Q5 recovers). This is the same small-sample-deflation pattern exp-20260615-023/exp-20260628-015 showed for the sibling volume-precursor signal (4.7x shrinkage at full population). Conclusion: the n=6 forward signal was noise, not a real de-allocation edge.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not build an ATR-extension (entry_exhaustion) de-rank / notional-haircut / sizing rule; do not sweep the 4.0 stretched threshold, ATR/MA/high lookbacks, top-N, hold, or cooldown; do not re-slice the 41 forward rows (exp-026 boundary still holds). The in-sample large-N surface is now also covered -- another in-sample extension cut is a repeat.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Only a materially different, PIT, NON-price field that predicts which extended breakouts fail (borrow/options/flow), OR closed forward rows on the EXACT accepted-sleeve trade sub-population large enough (>= the 8-row stretched floor) to test the narrow sleeve-only claim that the broad screen cannot refute -- which remains low-prior given the broad effect is absent.
