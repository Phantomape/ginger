# Experiment Card: exp-20260826-001

## Summary

Wire the missing borrow-coverage step into the daily pipeline: the pair forward-readiness borrow gate needs iBorrowDesk evidence archived at or before each batch first_seen_at and at most 3 calendar days old, but nothing in run.py fetches the day's short-side exposure tickers before the exposure observer freezes first_seen_at, and the Step 1.65 shard rotation skips microcaps outside build_default_refresh_universe, so batches structurally fail-close on missing/stale_pit_borrow (blocked_downstream_check: news_propagation_pair_forward_readiness measurement_ready_batches, pre-repair gate value 0; latest batch pair-ready-81e8043a1b900c1f45c1114c is blocked solely by 3 missing plus 21 stale borrow tickers with cross-side overlap already clear).
