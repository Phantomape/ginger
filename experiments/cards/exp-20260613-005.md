## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? First accepted in the regime-router line (exp-20260606-022 -> 027 ->
  this). The 1.5x tilt on mixed|balanced|normal rows lifted the sleeve overlay aggregate EV +0.0804
  (+1.0%) and PnL +$1,873 with all three windows improved and zero regressed. Both concentration
  guards passed at 20.9% (TOST) — the same screen that killed the consensus cell (APP 51.4% in
  exp-20260612-018/020) — confirming the ex-top-ticker screen of exp-20260612-027 selected a
  genuinely diversified cell. The predicted weak spot held exactly: late_strong EV delta is +0.0013
  (PnL +$31), so the cell earns its edge in calm mid-trend and weak tape, not in strong trend —
  consistent with exp-20260612-007's mirror finding that strong tape washes out regime conditioning.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Tuning the accepted variant on the frozen windows
  (no scalar sweep beyond 1.25/1.5, no cell-definition widening); promoting any second
  exp-20260612-027 cell without its own ex-top-ticker screen and reservation; re-attempting the
  rejected consensus-cell or compression-cell tilts.
- What new evidence would justify a retry / promotion? Closed forward default-off rows of the sleeve
  tagged with entry-time market state, showing the in-state edge persists out-of-sample (this pass
  is partly in-sample — the cell was screened on these same windows). Materiality is small (+1.0%,
  +$1,873) because the standalone overlay is 47 rows; a larger payoff needs the source earning
  allocator slots (currently 0, blocked by daily-top1 priority) or more independent clean cells
  stacked into a real router, each reserved separately.
