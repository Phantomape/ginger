## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The regime conditioning direction was sound: the best variant
  (mixed|balanced|normal cell, 1.5x tilt) lifted aggregate EV +0.7366 (+7.4%) and PnL +$15,167
  with all three windows improved, zero regressed, and max drawdown improved in every window.
  It failed exactly one predeclared guard: APP alone carries 51.38% of in-cell positive PnL
  versus the 50% single-ticker cap. The share is scalar-invariant (a uniform tilt rescales all
  in-cell rows equally), so no tested scalar could pass. Predicted top failure mode hit.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Re-running this tilt with relaxed concentration
  caps, other uniform scalars, or merged/nearby state cells on the frozen windows; relabeling
  the cell via regime_engine fields to dodge the frozen 606-022 classifier.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? (a) A per-ticker tilt cap (in-cell per-ticker
  notional ceiling or max tilted rows per ticker) predeclared as its own single hypothesis, or
  (b) forward closed consensus-sleeve rows in this state that dilute APP below 50% of in-cell
  positive PnL.
