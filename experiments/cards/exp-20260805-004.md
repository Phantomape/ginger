# Experiment Card: exp-20260805-004

## Summary

Massive grouped-daily bar freshness is a silent single point of failure for the dividend-restart forward settlement chain: the warehouse froze at 2026-07-24 for 12 days while the daily observer/settlement kept reporting status=ok alert=false, because (1) no bounded incremental grouped-daily catch-up is wired into the daily run before the observer/settlement consumers and (2) the settlement/observer have no fail-closed staleness check anchored to the data calendar (canonical OHLCV latest completed session). Repair: wire a bounded incremental massive catch-up into run.py ahead of the observer, and make the settlement degrade to alert=true stale status when bars lag the latest completed session by >=2 sessions. Fault recovery of a starving forward face per AGENTS.md section 6; does not count toward saturation.
