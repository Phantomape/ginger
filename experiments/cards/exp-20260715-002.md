## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The corrected `$10k` funded candidate sleeves often
  added value versus cash, but none earned enough to cover the opportunity cost
  of replacing 10% of the active core. The best candidate also breached the
  ES95 cap and materially regressed in two windows.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Any weight, threshold, hold,
  notional, subtype, ordering, additive-exposure, or double-scaling retry on the
  same 31 frozen representatives.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A prospective first-seen ledger, a
  complete pre-frozen selection panel, a genuinely new candidate family/data
  source, or a separately authorized risk-budget gate that explicitly permits
  return sacrifice for tail-risk reduction.
