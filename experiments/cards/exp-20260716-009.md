## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The old positive additive result did not survive
  executable capital. Late-strong and mid-weak lost EV and PnL, while only
  old-thin improved. Ten core-priority liquidations also crystallized ETF
  losses before some later core orders; in eight cases the released ETF cash
  was still insufficient to preserve the original core order without a small
  overdraft. The sleeve therefore was not a pure residual-cash allocation.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not change ETF notional, cash
  buffer, hold, SMA/momentum threshold, universe, forced-exit rule, or the
  greater-than-10-percent hurdle and rerun these frozen windows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Materially more settled forward
  replacement-value rows under the unchanged shared paper sleeve, or a new
  independent capital-allocation gate with a predeclared funding source that
  never relies on future core cash demand. A lower baseline alone is not new
  evidence.
