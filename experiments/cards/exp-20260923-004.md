# Experiment Card: exp-20260923-004

## Summary

Contract review of the exp-20260826-001 news-propagation pair forward readiness gate consumed by the exp-20260827-001 pair sleeve, triggered by identity-only evidence: since the exp-20260913-007 leg-priceability amendment took effect on 2026-09-14 every one of the 13 subsequent first-seen batches (2026-09-15..2026-09-23) is status=blocked, 7 of them solely by per-ticker borrow blockers on 2-5 short-side tickers out of 20-81 (BAO/DBIM/EVON/GST/BAGZ/BLTG/GRAY/MOT/LILW/SBEV/HWEP) which the exposure observer targeted prefetch did attempt on the batch day and iBorrowDesk answered not_found (unlisted/OTC sic_peer_index symbols, the same symbols exp-20260913-007 already excludes as unpriceable at admission), so the readiness gate fail-closes whole batches on tickers the admission rule would exclude anyway and the >=20-settled-basket acceptance contract is unreachable (6 ready of 32 batches lifetime, 1 of 13 since 09-14, 0 since 09-15). Amendment, forward-only for batches first seen at or after 2026-09-23T17:00:00+00:00: (a) a short-side ticker without valid PIT borrow evidence (missing/stale/invalid/unavailable) no longer blocks the batch; it is recorded in borrow_coverage.uncovered_tickers with its blocker kind under coverage_rule_version news_propagation_pair_borrow_coverage_v2 and the batch stays measurement_ready unless a batch-level blocker (mixed_first_seen_at, missing polarity, cross_side_ticker_overlap, side concentration) applies or no short-side ticker is covered (new blocker short_side_no_borrow_coverage); (b) the sleeve admits only covered short-side tickers, recording excluded_no_pit_borrow on the short leg and renormalizing the equal row weights over the kept tickers exactly as the exp-20260913-007 priceability exclusion does, and a side that empties is not admitted. Both conditions are wired into the existing run.py daily pipeline pass (news_event_exposure_observer -> news_propagation_pair_forward_observer.run and news_propagation_pair_paper_sleeve.run, the same code path replay uses) so the amended gate applies automatically in every daily run with no further IDs. Batches first seen before the effective timestamp keep the frozen v1 semantics byte-for-byte (persisted records are never rewritten or re-evaluated), no basket is admitted retroactively, H10 / 45bp per leg / USD 1000 per leg / 40% row-weight cap / >=20 baskets / >=10 dates / both halves / <=40% ticker share are unchanged, iBorrowDesk stays indicative not a locate, and no basket PnL or outcome field is read.
