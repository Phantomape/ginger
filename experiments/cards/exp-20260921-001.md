# Experiment Card: exp-20260921-001

## Summary

The Massive dividend-restart forward observer groups the v1 dividends chain by the literal feed spelling of ticker, but the provider emits the same instrument under two spellings over time (class shares XXXA versus XXX.A; preferreds XXXPRY versus XXXpY), so a spelling switch is misread as a 1095-day dividend gap or a no-prior first positive declaration: 332 of the 486 persisted forward candidates are such spelling-split artifacts (0 of them gate-eligible, 0 decisions affected, historical 89-row roster unaffected). Repair: before gap detection map each feed spelling to its instrument_master-canonical ticker when the literal spelling is absent from instrument_master and exactly one class or preferred variant spelling is present (ambiguous or absent stays literal, fail closed), group histories by the canonical ticker, record the feed spellings on the candidate row, and treat an existing literal decision key as known for its canonical form so nothing is re-emitted. Acceptance is correct implementation of the frozen candidate identity verified by synthetic unit tests plus an outcome-blind dry-run identity projection over the persisted history; it is not judged by any decision count or value.
