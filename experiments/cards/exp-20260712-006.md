## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The old daily Sharpe evidence was not auditable:
  full-slot days could record stale realized equity, final liquidation costs
  were absent from the last return, saved artifacts dropped the curve, and no
  complete historical trial panel existed. Correct MTM plus retained returns
  makes single-strategy PSR computable, while the missing selection pool
  correctly leaves DSR unavailable.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Never derive DSR from rounded
  `sharpe_daily`, `prior_trial_count`, a selected strategy's standard error, a
  hand-written Gate-5 summary, or current-working-stack PSR relabelled as the
  archived champion. Do not claim system-wide live enforcement from the one
  codified full-stack verdict.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A prospective complete selection
  pool containing every attempted configuration, a declared expected
  return-date vector, persisted per-trial hashes/source locators, and the same
  window/data/cost/return-basis context. Stronger enforcement additionally
  needs the expected date vector bound to the exact Gate-4 artifact and the
  reusable DSR gate wired into each legacy/manual live activation path.
