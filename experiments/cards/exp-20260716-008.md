## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The queue produced only 12 delayed fills. Old-thin improved, but late-strong and mid-weak both lost EV and PnL; aggregate EV fell from 6.2057 to 5.6413 and aggregate PnL fell by $7,804.17. FIFO, price-thesis, concentration, and cash-conservation checks were clean, so the realized failure mode was stale-signal displacement rather than a bookkeeping defect.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retune FIFO/LIFO priority, queue age, expiry, cash-shortfall size, or stop/target invalidation on these same frozen outcomes.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different gate shape or data source, or materially more settled forward cash-conflict decisions under a predeclared policy; the 12 fills here are insufficient.
