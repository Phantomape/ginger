## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The source and mapping cleared the outcome-blind density contract, but weekly owned-domain attention did not identify issuers whose ten-session returns paid for displaced core capital. Only `mid_weak` had positive standalone sleeve PnL, while the capital-neutral mix lost PnL in all three windows; `old_thin` and `late_strong` also trailed cash, SPY, and QQQ replacement values.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Retuning the story floor, prior-week lookback, acceleration threshold, top-k, hold length, notional, issuer subset, or response function on this reconstructed Algolia archive.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? An immutable as-published HN source that materially changes historical membership, or at least 30 newly settled append-only forward issuer-week rows across at least 10 tickers under a predeclared new gate shape.
