## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The additive diagnostic gained `+0.1033` EV and `+$1,140.27`, but the gain was late-window-driven. The 41 issuer legs were only six independent quarterly release decisions, top-five positive-PnL concentration was `76.11%`, and the added `$10,000` basket had no modeled cash source.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retune issuer identity, serious definition, case/volume guards, delta threshold, rank/top-N, entry, hold, notional, or cost on the same frozen quarters; do not count issuer legs as independent decisions.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? At least 30 independent prospectively settled unchanged-policy quarterly decisions (at least 10 per standard window), an explicit cash-conserving funding/displacement contract, and an effective-dated sponsor/product-exposure relation; otherwise an independent PIT source or genuinely different gate shape.
