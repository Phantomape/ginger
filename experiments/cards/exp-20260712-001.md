## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The broker SDK already exposed stable identifiers, prices, fees and full timestamps, but the adapter discarded them after each run. Reusing the existing daily session made persistence low-risk. One CANCELLED deal and six history-window baseline mismatches forced versioned latest-effective projection and strict quarantine; the reconciled subset still yielded 87 trusted closed lifecycles.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not reserve new IDs for routine refreshes, duplicate order-level fees per fill, fabricate historical cash snapshots, use `position_id` as lot identity, or synthesize fills to force reconciliation.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Verified broker timezone/push semantics, corporate-action or transfer records, a demonstrated cross-zero allocation contract, per-collection commit/visibility proof, or cursor-based cash-flow backfill evidence. Exit-side drift consumption belongs in a separate experiment using the 87 trusted lifecycles.
