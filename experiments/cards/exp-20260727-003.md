## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The parser and append-only ledger were sound, but production had no default producer; an old `status=ok` summary therefore concealed fourteen days of source starvation.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not backdate missed vintages, treat action/report dates as availability, reinclude DoD/USACE, relax freshness, retune eligibility, or infer alpha from unsettled rows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Another producer ID requires a real source/schema/retrieval-clock/health regression; alpha remains parked until the frozen settlement and replacement-value thresholds pass.
