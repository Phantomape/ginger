## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The raw covariance term lowered overlapping risk and drawdown in every window, but it also cut profitable common exposure. Final executed shares changed on 38 entries and released cash added eight trades, yet winner clipping dominated avoided losses and new-trade gains.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not sweep lookback, covariance floors, cross-term thresholds, scalar caps/floors, active-position subsets, residualization, or requested-versus-cash-scaled notional variants on these same canonical outcomes.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? An unseen out-of-sample window with an independently predeclared gate, at least +50% and +10 new settled forward active-book decisions, or a genuinely new non-price dependence source.
