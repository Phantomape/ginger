# exp-20260615-019 PIT Regime-State Classifier + Attribution

Status: `observed_only`  Lane: `measurement_repair`

## Headline finding

Both an accepted sleeve (Fundamental Growth + RS) and the rejected
deferred-revenue scout (exp-20260615-017) lose money specifically in the
`choppy_range` regime (FGRS −$197.56/trade, 27% win; deferred −$179.36/trade,
17% win) while staying clearly positive in BOTH `risk_on_trend` and
`risk_off_stress`. The loss axis is therefore directionless chop (low bull,
low stress), NOT stress: Spearman(`risk_off_score`, PnL) is ~0 (FGRS −0.044,
deferred +0.085), so a naive monotonic "cut exposure in risk-off" tilt would
not help. The useful gate construct is a chop indicator (low `bull_score` AND
low `risk_off_score`).

Caveats (why this is a LEAD, not yet actionable):
- Not a clean 3-window confirmation. The `choppy_range` loss shows in
  `late_strong` and `old_thin` (the windows that had chop samples) but
  `mid_weak` was classified ~entirely risk-on, so it had ~0-1 chop trades.
- Chop-bucket samples are modest (FGRS 16-20/window; deferred 5-7/window).
- In `old_thin`, even `risk_off_stress` was roughly flat/negative, so that
  window was broadly hard outside risk-on.
- Next step is a portfolio-level SOFT chop down-tilt validated on forward
  state-tagged replacement-value rows, never a hard per-window on/off gate
  (exp-20260614-010 showed a hard frozen-window breadth filter regresses all
  three windows).

## What was built

A mechanical, point-in-time regime classifier (probability over `risk_on_trend / choppy_range / risk_off_stress`, plus `risk_off_score` and `bull_score`) from free index OHLCV + breadth, and read-only conditional attribution of accepted-sleeve replay trades by entry-day regime. Zero execution change.
