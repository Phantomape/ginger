## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The dated endpoint covered every fixed decision date, while three pages exposed duplicate descriptive vendor revisions. A 16:00 America/New_York decision cutoff plus future-version quarantine preserved the eligible identity, exact raw bytes, and unique `(as_of,ticker)` membership without admitting future metadata into candidate decisions.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not reserve another identity/materialization ID merely to add month-start dates, consume descriptive metadata, change minimum-row thresholds, or retune the same revision-collapse rule.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Identity may reopen only for an independently verified as-published vintage or a genuine cross-page/source-format integrity fault. The alpha path is instead a fixed outcome-blind D0-D3 panel, model-diverse debate, and a separate promoted `private_replay_scout`.
