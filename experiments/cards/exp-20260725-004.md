# Experiment Card: exp-20260725-004

## Summary

Fault/wiring repair: the core drawdown flow-put stabilization observer reads data/non_ohlcv/options_forward/options_collection_quality_gate.json at sleeve build time, but quant/run.py only rebuilds that quality gate in _refresh_options_forward_ledger_after_quant_signals AFTER all sleeves are built, so the sleeve's own asof quote-date row is always absent when the sleeve runs (07-24 evidence: chain written 03:07Z with 5902 rows/60 tickers, sleeve ran 03:33:59Z and recorded options_quality_status=missing_quality_row with options_scoring_allowed=false, quality row for 2026-07-24 written 03:34:54Z with scoring_allowed=true). daily_options_complete_signals is therefore structurally pinned at 0 and the lane's 20-independent-closed-decisions reopen counter can never accumulate. Repair: add a reusable quality-gate-only refresh entrypoint to scripts/run_options_forward_ledger.py (same _collection_quality_gate thresholds and writer) and call it in quant/run.py after the non-OHLCV snapshot/chain fetch and before sleeve builds, keeping fail-closed semantics when the chain or quality row is genuinely bad.
