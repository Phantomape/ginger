## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The inline ledger sees add-on fills and
  marked-value position sizing compounding: the champion books up to ~$189k
  more entry basis than settled cash at peaks (8x deeper than the
  exp-20260715-005 closed-rows reconstruction), so enforcing executable cash
  removes roughly half the canonical PnL; old_thin EV nearly vanishes
  (1.31 -> 0.11).
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Re-tuning the enforcement
  (partial-fill vs reject policy, cash buffers, per-order caps) or reserving
  another audit-only quantification of the same overdraft surface — the
  phenomenon is measured in-engine on every run now.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Not a retry but a follow-up
  decision ticket: flip `CASH_LEDGER_ENFORCED` default, republish a
  frozen-input post-cash-ledger Gate-1 anchor, and re-open the parked
  capital-allocation lanes whose displacement math was biased by the
  unenforced champion. Live claims additionally need production runner
  cash/sizing semantics compared against the backtest scale-down rule.
