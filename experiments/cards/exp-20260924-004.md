# Experiment Card: exp-20260924-004

## Summary

Contract review of the exp-20260824-002 / exp-20260826-001 news-propagation pair forward readiness gate consumed by the exp-20260827-001 pair sleeve, triggered by identity-only evidence one day after exp-20260923-004 (which pre-registered cross_side_overlap_remains_dominant_batch_blocker as a main failure mode): the first v2 record (2026-09-24 nightly) is measurement_ready and admitted a basket, but under the v2 borrow rule every remaining post-2026-09-14 block (6 of 14 records: 09-15T16, 09-16T03, 09-16T16, 09-17T03, 09-17T16, 09-21T18) carries cross_side_ticker_overlap as its only non-borrow blocker (10 of 33 lifetime), and the overlap content is whole sic_peer groups (semiconductors 23-33 tickers, autos 17, China internet / AI software 14-20) placed on both sides because one negative and one positive event of the same peer group land in the same batch - not noise symbols. Removing the overlap tickers from BOTH sides leaves both sides non-empty in 6/6 post-09-14 cases (long 19-35 / short 25-81 tickers) with residual max ticker row weight <= 0.087 versus the 0.40 cap, so the gate fail-closes whole batches whose unambiguous-polarity residual is admissible and the >=20-settled-basket acceptance contract accrues at roughly half the reachable cadence (hypothetical 18/33 ready lifetime and 8/14 since 09-14 under v2 alone; 25/33 and 14/14 with overlap exclusion). Amendment, forward-only for batches first seen at or after 2026-09-24T17:00:00+00:00: (a) the observer excludes overlap tickers from both sides for readiness evaluation, keeps cross_side_ticker_overlap as a list, adds overlap_exclusion {rule_version news_propagation_pair_cross_side_overlap_exclusion_v2, excluded_tickers, long_side_residual, short_side_residual}, evaluates the 40% concentration blockers and the v2 borrow coverage on the residual sides, and blocks only on long_side_emptied_by_overlap / short_side_emptied_by_overlap or the other batch-level blockers; (b) the sleeve removes the excluded tickers from both legs, records excluded_cross_side_overlap on each leg, renormalises the equal row weights over the kept tickers exactly as exp-20260913-007 and exp-20260923-004 do, and never admits an emptied side. Both are wired into the existing run.py daily pass (the same code replay uses). Batches first seen before the effective timestamp keep the current semantics byte-for-byte (no persisted record or basket rewritten, no retroactive admission), acceptance counting restarted 2026-09-14 continues unchanged for overlap-free batches, H10 / 45bp / USD 1000 / 40% / >=20 / >=10 unchanged, iBorrowDesk stays indicative, no outcome or PnL field is read.
