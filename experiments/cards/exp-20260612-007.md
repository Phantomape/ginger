## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The right tail exists but is short-lived and regime-dependent.
  5-day extensions at near_high>=0.95 added +0.823 aggregate EV (+5.19%) and +$10,348 PnL from
  late_strong/mid_weak continuation, but old_thin regressed (EV -0.124, PnL -$2,355: giveback in
  weak tape), and 10-day extensions destroyed value outright (-5.8% / -6.1% aggregate EV). Every
  variant regressed at least one window, so neither the standard zero-regression Gate 4 nor the
  AGENTS.md >10% state-surface EV bar was met. Predicted failure mode (window_instability /
  extension_giveback_reversal) hit; capacity blocking never bound (0 blocked entries).
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Near-high expiry extensions with nearby thresholds
  (0.85-1.0) or extension lengths (3-15 trading days) on the frozen three windows; trailing-stop /
  MFE-giveback conversions of the same idea (exp-20260503-009, exp-20260502-014 already rejected);
  unconditional hold changes (exp-20260518-001).
- What new evidence would justify a retry? (a) Closed forward default-off rows showing post-expiry
  continuation attribution on live snapshots (SNXX/CRDO-style rank-1 exits after 2026-06), or
  (b) a regime/tail-state gate that provably disables the extension in weak tape (old_thin-like),
  tested as its own single hypothesis against the accepted exp-20260520-001 baseline.
