## Summary

Measurement repair of the broad OHLCV warehouse hot tier consumed by the news-propagation pair sleeve priceability gate (exp-20260913-007), the news_event_exposure_observer, the entity_theme forward observer and every other warehouse reader: mid-session run.py passes on 2026-09-15, 09-16, 09-17, 09-18 and 09-21 (started 09:02-11:07 local) ran refresh_warehouse_ohlcv, which fetches with end=now and upserts with update_existing=False, so 6583 intraday snapshot bars (partial-session close/high/low/volume; close gap vs massive daily_bars over 0.3 percent on 711-1132 of 1309 compared tickers per session vs 0 on 09-23) were frozen for the whole 1304-ticker refresh universe plus the 56-ticker core batch, and the evening pass could never correct them; separately yfinance served no 2026-09-22 bar to the 09-22/09-23 evening passes for 1162 tickers (self-healing now that the vendor serves it). Repair: (1) refresh_warehouse_ohlcv truncates every fetched frame to dates at or before latest_completed_us_equity_session(run_clock) before the split guard and upsert (new run_clock argument, summary fields completed_session and dropped_incomplete_session_rows); (2) one-off re-fetch of exactly the frozen before-census (ticker, date) set plus the 1162 hole tickers on 09-22 through the same vendor path (data_layer.get_ohlcv_many) and rewrite under source ohlcv_warehouse_refresh:yfinance:intraday_bar_repair:<id> with update_existing=True; no other row written, no settled outcome rewritten, no basket re-admitted.
