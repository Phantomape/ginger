## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The existing pilot supplied a concrete schema, while adversarial tests exposed the real integration hazards: same-publisher pseudo-independence, nested permission escalation, Markdown marker injection, stale digest timestamps, and the UTC/local research-date boundary. A strict registry, validator, hash-bound scan manifest, publication check, and negative tests closed those paths without touching strategy behavior.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not wire another LLM or investment-research skill directly into security ranking, strategy rules, experiment reservation, or trading; do not treat a skill name, join, theme basket, or narrative bottleneck as a new EvidenceSurface or alpha result.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Bind the post-generation veto to a canonical history snapshot hash and cutoff, or introduce a genuinely different bounded generator with its own version/hash, independent-source contract, and zero-permission tests. Splitting Desktop automations is external wiring, not a validator retune.
