## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The standalone spread lost money in all three windows. Gross PnL was `-$237.63`; `$342.00` of trading costs mattered, but only `$4.64` of borrow cost was charged. The failure is therefore the pressure-to-relative-return mapping, not an inflated borrow-fee model. Funding the sleeve from a very strong core amplified the portfolio loss.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not sweep top-N, correlation floor/lookback, clusters, hold, cooldown, notional, costs, borrow accrual, direction, or the 10% weight on the same ORTEX/Moomoo rows. Do not relabel the same rows as squeeze-long or another consensus filter.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely new gate shape or signal family, a sustainable same-day cross-sectional feed with materially more prospectively settled rows, or new PIT availability/utilization data plus broker locate evidence.
