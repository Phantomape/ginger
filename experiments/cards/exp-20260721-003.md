## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The loose addition population included internal
  S&P 500/400/600 tier migrations.  The preliminary net-new/inverse-volatility
  correction exposed a 36.93% `mid_weak` top-sector weight versus the locked
  35% ceiling.  Separately, the official website terms do not authorize using
  its content to develop or support an investment strategy, and the historical
  view does not prove immutable publication vintages.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not add a sector cap, relax
  35%, substitute equal-dollar weights, include tier migrations, or treat a
  supplied manifest date as a verified PIT clock on these frozen windows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Explicit written permission or a
  different authorized immutable source with auditable publication vintages,
  plus a genuinely new evidence axis rather than a response retune.
