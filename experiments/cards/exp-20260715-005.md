# exp-20260715-005 — Explicit-cash low-deployment ETF gate

## Decision hypothesis

Replay the unchanged accepted low-deployment ETF selector through a dated cash
ledger. Core exits and entries have priority; an open ETF is liquidated at the
same-day open when necessary, and a new ETF entry can use only remaining cash,
up to the frozen $10,000 cap.

## Locked policy

- ETF universe: QQQ, SPY, IWM, GLD, SLV.
- Positive SMA200 state and positive 20-session momentum.
- Trigger only with at most one active core position.
- Next-open entry, scheduled 10-session close, one ETF position.
- Existing execution costs, no leverage, no core displacement.
- Gate 4 requires aggregate EV improvement above 10%; thresholds were not
  changed after observing results.

## Result

Rejected. All three windows improved, with aggregate EV 12.2698 to 12.4418
(+1.40%) and aggregate PnL +$2,827.84. Worst drawdown drift was +0.0487pp.
The ETF produced 17 executed lifecycles, including six core-priority early
exits. However, the reconstructed core ledger generated 18/19/20 negative-cash
events across the three windows and minimum cash reached roughly -$166k to
-$189k. The EV gain also missed the predeclared >10% hurdle.

## Reflection

The ETF edge remains directionally positive after imposing priority and cash
admission, but this experiment cannot establish a cash-only portfolio because
the canonical core backtest sizes against risk/equity without settled-cash
debits. The largest regret is that historical acceptance treated standalone
notional as deployable capital; the remaining unknown is the ETF's incremental
value after the core allocator itself becomes cash-conserving.

Forbidden retry: do not sweep ETF cap, hold period, universe, SMA/momentum
thresholds, or lower the >10% acceptance hurdle on these frozen windows.

New evidence required: a shared core allocator that schedules actual fill-date
cash reservations (including dated add-ons and partial reductions) and either
scales or rejects unaffordable core orders before ranking the ETF on genuinely
remaining cash. That is a core capital-allocation gate, not another ETF retune.
