## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? CPSC was positive in aggregate (+$1,583.24) but had only eight settled trades and regressed in `old_thin`; NHTSA lost $1,736.87, more than offsetting it. The pooled policy therefore had EV -0.0188 and PnL -$153.64 and also missed comparator and concentration bars.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retry the fixed recipe by changing one source, issuer map, point-in-time date, green/SPY confirmation, rank, cooldown, holding period, notional, cost, or window at a time. Do not spend one experiment ID per additional official source.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? At least three newly audit-ready official sources, each spanning all three canonical windows and contributing at least 20 expected settled trades, consumed together in one batch; alternatively, a materially different gate shape.
