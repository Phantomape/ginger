# Experiment Card: exp-20260717-002

## Summary

The daily sleeve-health report's disk-freshness reader only recognizes snapshots.jsonl, *summary.json, and heartbeat-contract state.json, but seven newer default-off surfaces persist via latest_snapshot.json (treasury_auction_weak_demand_tbt fresh as of 2026-07-15 with 112 candidates, pcaob_form_ap fresh 2026-07-16) and one via a dateless state.json, so alive sleeves are reported as never_persisted stalls; additionally a retired sleeve (finra_iwm, build_status retired_default_off_paper_disabled) is still counted stalled. This makes the daily stalled_sleeves warning a standing false-positive list that trains operators to ignore real stalls. Teaching the reader to (a) include *snapshot.json date surfaces and (b) exclude retired-status sleeves from stalled restores the report's signal.
