# Experiment Card: exp-20260811-001

## Summary

Genuine settlement-pipeline defect (not routine delta materialization): the run.py-wired estimate-revision outcome settlement builder filters its population to candidate-overlap rows only, a semantic population-filter defect that structurally starves the exp-20260721-002 phase-2 readiness contract (12 of 1299 qualified independent decisions ever received outcome rows; settled h5/h10/h20 = 4/3/0 against the 30/30/30 bar, mathematically unreachable at ~11 overlap decisions per 3 weeks).
