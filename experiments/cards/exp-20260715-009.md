## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The slow aggregate-share sign was directionally harmful in every window. The 0.90 negative bucket cut many large winners (including XOM, COIN, and JPM), removing $9,443.76 in aggregate, while existing position caps allowed only 7 material positive increases versus 28 material negative haircuts.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not change the 1.10/0.90 scalars, use only one sign, or retune the 20-series minimum, report gap/recency, split tolerance, fund/ticker subset, or position cap on these frozen windows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different N-PORT signal with an explicit mechanism, such as fund-flow-normalized ownership breadth rather than aggregate-share sign, plus an unseen out-of-sample window or materially new settled forward evidence; otherwise switch data sources.
