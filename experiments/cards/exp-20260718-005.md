## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The authenticated source and unchanged policy were
  valid, but the 2,580-row ORTEX surface produced only six eligible stress
  transitions (two in each canonical window), and all six missed every generated
  fresh-core candidate. The gate therefore changed no trades and supplied no
  incremental expected value.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not lower the 1.0% fee level,
  change the 0.25pp/0.70 compound branch, shorten or lengthen the ten-session
  cooldown, change the one-session admission response, reslice issuers, or use
  the fee-delta branch without historical availability. Those are same-surface
  retunes after Gate 4 rejection.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? Reopen this entry-admission surface
  only with populated PIT availability/utilization that can activate the frozen
  compound branch, or with materially more PIT rows satisfying the repository
  row-growth rule and demonstrating at least five actual fresh-core candidate
  touches in every canonical window (15 aggregate) before another price replay.
