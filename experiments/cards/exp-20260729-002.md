# Experiment Card: exp-20260729-002

## Summary

Fault recovery: the daily FINRA short-interest coverage row is generated before the active source producer refreshes the archive, so same-run health is one cycle stale and historical unique-ticker count hides latest-settlement cohort sparsity. Refreshing the authorized archive against the existing broad-ingest universe before coverage registration, plus exposing latest-settlement cohort density, should make same-run PIT health truthful without changing signals, ranking, sizing, or orders.
