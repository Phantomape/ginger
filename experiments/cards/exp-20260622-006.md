## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? The 13F co-ownership graph selected laggards that were stale or index-overlap-heavy rather than true near-term peer-reaction candidates. The coownership overlay was net-negative versus core and materially worse than the accepted rolling-correlation peer definition.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not retry by loosening `min_coownership_lift`, `min_shared_managers`, sector/industry cohold filters, top-N, notional, hold days, or response curves on the same static 13F coownership graph and canonical windows.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A genuinely different ownership relation, such as PIT holdings-change co-accumulation among active managers, concentration-weighted high-conviction edges, non-quarterly ownership flow, or materially new forward closed rows.
