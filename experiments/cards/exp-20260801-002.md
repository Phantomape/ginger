## Reflection

The current producer summary exposed a natural boundary regression:
`pending_job_validation_status=expired` stopped the 2026-08-01 run before a
fresh request could recover USAspending forward rows. The repair stayed local
to expired receipt handling and preserved the existing fail-closed contracts.

Forbidden near-neighbor retry: do not open another USAspending receipt-retry
ticket for ordinary expired async jobs, status poll exhaustion, or cross-date
pending continuation; those paths are now covered by the receipt journal tests.

New evidence required: a future repair needs a new reproducible USAspending
producer boundary, such as an upstream schema/API contract change,
completed-journal proof mismatch, immutable snapshot validation failure, or a
natural daily run that still ends stale despite a valid fresh request path.
