## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? At the first executable open after Schedule TO, 10 of 16 rejected candidates had no positive spread after 5% carry, 35 bp round-trip cost, and the $20 action fee; five failed the 70% implied-completion floor and one had discounted offer value at or below break. Only HEES entered and earned $113.99 after a higher bid, leaving 100% PnL concentration and too little return to compensate for reserving 10% away from the strong core.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not sweep completion probability, carry, event fee, lookback, notional, ADV, break-loss budget, or window thresholds on this same frozen SC TO-T surface.
- What new evidence would justify a retry? A different contractual corporate-action family, verified Moomoo tender cutoff/fee/whole-share execution evidence, or materially more settled forward cash-tender rows under the unchanged policy.
