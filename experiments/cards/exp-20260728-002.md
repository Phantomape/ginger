## Post-Run Reflection

- Why did the result happen? Massive supplied the required date-native market rows and inactive listings, while real payloads exposed two identity hazards: case-sensitive provider symbols and FIGIs spanning more than one ticker/listing lifecycle. Preserving provider case and binding identity to listing boundaries removed both collisions.
- Which near-neighbor retry is now forbidden? Do not reserve another Massive source-plumbing ID merely to change pagination, storage fields, or thresholds while the same resumable backfill completes.
