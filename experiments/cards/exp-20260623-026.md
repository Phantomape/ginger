# Experiment Card: exp-20260623-026

## Summary

Held-book risk-budget overlay: applying a PIT risk-contribution cap (cap any single name at 15% of ex-ante portfolio risk) plus a 30% annualized vol target (excess to cash), rebalanced monthly using ONLY trailing 90d covariance, improves risk-adjusted return (Sharpe up, max-drawdown down) at acceptable return cost versus static buy-and-hold of the same held names, aggregated over the three standard windows. Motivated by the read-only diagnostic (scripts/probe_held_book_concentration.py): MUU alone is 34% of portfolio risk and the only real de-risk lever is raising cash, not rotating among correlated high-beta names. Observed-only policy simulation on the held universe; no core-strategy/order/ranking change; discretionary risk policy if it wins.
