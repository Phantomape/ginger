# Experiment Card: exp-20260601-007

## Summary

Short-horizon cross-sectional reversal: on the broad 1446-ticker all-windows-full-liquid universe, stocks with the most negative recent formation return (1/3/5-day losers) out-return the biggest winners over the next 5-10 trading days, measured with a one-day skip to avoid bid-ask-bounce contamination. The losers-minus-winners long-short daily spread should be positive with a t-stat >= 2 across sampled days, monotonic across formation quintiles, robust in a majority of the canonical 3 windows, and survive a round-trip transaction-cost estimate (2x ROUND_TRIP_COST_PCT = 0.70pct per rebalance).
