# Experiment Card: exp-20260713-008

## Summary

Tested a fixed mapped-sponsor ClinicalTrials.gov Phase 3 first-results candidate pool: issuer green and ahead of SPY on the first eligible session, top one per day, ten-session same-ticker cooldown, next-open entry, tenth-close exit, `$4,000`, and 35 bps costs.

- Status / decision: `rejected` / `rejected_clinicaltrials_phase3_results_candidate_pool`
- Exact history events: `156` across `9` eligible mapped tickers
- Trades: `47` across `9` tickers and all `3` canonical windows

## Gate Decision

Gate 1, source/sentinel Gate 2, and Gate 3 (`47 / 250 = 18.8%`) passed. Gate 4 failed because one window's EV regressed, contribution was too concentrated, the accepted-distribution PnL comparator was not beaten, and daily candidate lifecycle parity was incomplete. The daily production wiring was removed; no live/default policy behavior changed.

## Post-Run Reflection

- Why: the source was broad and profitable in every window, but averaged only about `$29.52` per target trade and relied heavily on five positive contributors.
- Forbidden retry: do not retune sponsor aliases/map, green or SPY-relative thresholds, rank, cooldown, hold, notional, costs, or window slices on this frozen source.
- New evidence required: a genuinely new clinical-results semantic direction or endpoint source with audited PIT labels, or at least `30` closed forward replacement-value rows.
