# Experiment Card: exp-20260913-007

## Summary

Contract review of the exp-20260827-001 news-propagation pair paper sleeve, triggered by identity-only evidence from exp-20260913-006: after the pricing repair 0 of 5 pending baskets can ever settle because every basket holds at least one leg ticker with no bar in any authorized price source (sic_peer_index symbols BAO/DBIM/EVON/HWEP/GTIJF absent from massive instrument_master, OTC ABIT/FMHS with zero bars, AIEV/BINI/ECDA delisted before their decision dates), so the frozen fail-closed missing-leg rule makes the forward acceptance contract (>=20 settled baskets over >=10 dates) structurally unreachable. Amendment: (a) admission gains an outcome-blind leg priceability condition - a side ticker is admitted only if a bar exists in warehouse-or-massive on every one of the 5 sessions strictly before the entry session; excluded tickers are recorded on the decision and the side's equal-row weights are renormalized over the remaining tickers, a side that empties is not admitted; (b) a pending basket whose exit session plus a 5-session grace period has passed while a leg is still unpriceable is terminalized as outcome_status=unsettleable with no PnL fields and never counts toward the acceptance contract. Both conditions are wired into the existing run.py daily pipeline pass (news_propagation_pair_paper_sleeve.run, the same code path replay uses) so admission filtering and terminalization happen automatically in every daily run with no further IDs. Frozen decisions are never rewritten; H10, 45bp/leg, USD 1000/leg, the 40% cap and the acceptance thresholds are unchanged; no basket PnL is read.
