# Experiment Card: exp-20260618-008

## Summary

Portfolio equity-curve adaptive sizing (anti-Martingale): when trailing equity is in drawdown >5% below its 20-day high-water mark, scale new entry notional to 0.7x. This is a portfolio-level risk allocation overlay that reduces exposure during adverse equity-curve regimes without changing entry/exit signals.
